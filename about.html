About Resonance

Welcome to Resonance, a cutting-edge music streaming platform developed by Team No. 21.

Our mission is to unlock the potential of human creativity by giving a million creative artists the opportunity to live off their art and billions of fans the opportunity to enjoy and be inspired by it.

Whether you're looking for deep cuts for late nights, high-energy beats for the gym, or a relaxing acoustic playlist, Resonance brings your favorite music all into one place.
